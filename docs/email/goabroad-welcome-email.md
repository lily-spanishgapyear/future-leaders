## Setting it up in Mailchimp

1. **Content → Email templates → Create template → Code your own → Paste in code.** Paste the full HTML file.
2. Every section has an `mc:edit` tag, so once you save the template you can click any text or image in the normal
   Mailchimp editor and change it without touching code.
3. **Swap the photos.** Every image is a labelled placeholder. Upload your photos to **Content Studio**, then click each
   placeholder and replace it. Sizes:
   - Logo: 360×90 transparent PNG
   - Hero: 1200×700 (your best big group photo, with everyone smiling)
   - Three program photos: 1000×600 each (a train or travel photo, summer teens, someone at an internship)
   - Photo strip: three 400×400 squares (food, a landmark, a candid laughing shot)
   - Your headshot: 160×160
4. **Add a real testimonial** in the dark quote box. Use a real quote, and get permission if it came from a private message.
5. **Check the reply button.** "Ask me anything" uses `*|LIST:EMAIL|*`, your audience's default from address. If replies
   should go to a different inbox, change that link to `mailto:you@yourdomain.com`.
6. **The "Which one sounds like you?" buttons** do double duty. After sending, go to **Reports → Click performance**
   to see who clicked which program, then tag or segment those people (gap semester, summer, placements) for follow-up
   emails that only talk about their program.
7. **First-name fallback:** if a lead has no first name, the greeting shows "Hi there," automatically.
8. Send a test to yourself on **desktop and phone**. The layout stacks into one column on mobile.

### Ideas for a short follow-up series
- **Day 3:** "A week in the life" photo story for whichever program they clicked
- **Day 7:** Meet the team (faces, plus why you started this)
- **Day 14:** Parent FAQ (safety, housing, credits, 529 plans) and an invite to book a call
