🎓 Gap Semester · Ages 18–22

Your gap semester in Spain

Taking time between high school and college (or a breather during it)? This is the big one. You'll spend about a month travelling Spain by train with your group, then settle into Barcelona and pick the path that fits you.

🚆  A month exploring Spain by train with your cohort
🏙️  Two months in Barcelona on an internship, entrepreneurship or special-interest pathway
🗣️  Spanish classes and lots of real-life practice
📚  Earn up to 12 college credits (Gap Year Association, through the University of Montana)
🍂  Fall and spring start dates

☀️ Summer · High school students

A summer in Spain

A month-long taste of independence for high schoolers. It's a safe, well-supported first trip abroad that also looks great on a college application (parents, this one's for you too 😉).

🏡  Week 1 in Madrid with a host family, Spanish classes and excursions
🌊  Weeks 2–4 in Barcelona with hands-on workshops and mentors
✨  Pick your track: Entrepreneurship, AI & Innovation or Film & Media
🤝  Make friends from all over and come home with a real project to show
